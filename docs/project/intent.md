# Intent

## Purpose
AI PrepInterviewer helps job candidates prepare for interviews by analysing their resume, recommending suitable roles, running AI-led mock interviews, evaluating their answers and tracking progress over time.

## Goals
- G1: Candidates get realistic mock interview practice for a chosen role.
- G2: Candidates get feedback on strengths and areas to improve.
- G3: Role recommendations and interview questions are based on the candidate's own resume.
- G4: Candidates can see their progress across completed interviews.
- G5: Candidates stay in control of their resume, interview choices and stored data.

## Not goals
- Guaranteeing real hiring outcomes; scores are practice estimates only.
- Replacing a human interviewer; the avatar is a visual representation of the AI interviewer.
- Later enhancements, deferred for now: lip-sync and expressive animation, adaptive question planning, detailed skill analytics, downloadable reports, administrator dashboard.

## Success
- A candidate can register, upload a resume, choose a role, complete an interview and get a saved report they can reopen later.
- Each candidate sees only their own resumes, interviews and reports.
- AI, database, file and speech-service failures show a clear message to the candidate.
